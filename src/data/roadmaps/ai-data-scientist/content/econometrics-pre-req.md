# Econometrics Prerequisites

## What you'll learn
Build intuition for statistical relationships, estimation, uncertainty, and the assumptions behind drawing conclusions from data.

### Topics to cover
- Random variables and probability distributions
- Sampling, estimation, and uncertainty
- Regression assumptions and interpretation
- Confounding, correlation versus causation, and bias

## Free learning resources
- [10 Fundamental Theorems for Econometrics](https://bookdown.org/ts_robinson1994/10EconometricTheorems/) — reference notes.
- [OpenIntro Statistics](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/) — free introductory statistics textbook.
- [Introduction to Econometrics with R](https://www.econometrics-with-r.org/) — free online textbook.

## Practice
1. Explain why correlation does not by itself establish causation.
2. Interpret a regression coefficient in a real-world example.
3. List assumptions that would need checking before interpreting a regression result causally.
