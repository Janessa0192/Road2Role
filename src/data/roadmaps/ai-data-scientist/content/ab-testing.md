# A/B Testing

## What you'll learn
A/B testing compares two versions of a product or experience to estimate whether a change affects a measurable outcome.

### Key concepts
- Control and treatment groups
- Hypotheses and success metrics
- Random assignment and experiment duration
- Statistical significance, confidence intervals, and practical significance
- Guardrail metrics and common experiment pitfalls
