# Differential Calculus

## What you'll learn
Understand rates of change and derivatives, which are important for optimization methods used in machine learning.

### Topics to cover
- Functions, limits, and derivatives
- Derivative rules and chain rule
- Partial derivatives and gradients
- How gradients relate to minimizing a loss function

## Free learning resources
- [Khan Academy: Differential Calculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/differential-calculus) — lessons and practice.
- [3Blue1Brown: Essence of Calculus](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/calculus) — visual intuition.
- [Mathematics for Machine Learning (free book)](https://mml-book.github.io/) — mathematical foundations including calculus and linear algebra.

## Practice
1. Differentiate a few simple polynomial functions.
2. Use the chain rule on a nested function.
3. Explain in your own words how a gradient can guide a model toward a lower loss.
